What this tool does

DPI checking, editable physical sizing, mirrored PNG export with DPI metadata, and up to 30 tiled A4/Letter PDF pages rasterized at 150 DPI. PNG output is limited to 8192 pixels per side and 32 MP. It is not a product-specific transfer template or a print-quality guarantee.

Check the result

Results depend on your inputs, browser support and the file formats used. Review the output before publishing, printing, submitting a form, paying someone or committing material to a project. This tool is provided as a practical aid, without a guarantee of a particular outcome.
